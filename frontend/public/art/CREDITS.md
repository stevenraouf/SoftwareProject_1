# AERIS window artwork

The aircraft-window surround and translucent cloud layer are original digital renders made for this design. The window and shade are built from a curved surface profile. The cloud shapes use a seeded density field.

The refined surround is a 1200 × 1520 transparent PNG, rendered from the original geometry. Light falls from the upper left, with a cool reflection on the inner edge and a soft contact shadow beneath the molding. The cloud image is a 1920 × 883 transparent PNG, rendered again from its density field. Both use the silhouettes from the earlier 600 × 760 surround and 1000 × 460 cloud artwork.

The destination photographs below were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons and cropped, resized and JPEG-compressed for the window. Each adaptation is provided under its stated source license. The photographs illustrate destination regions. The Haneda view shows the Fuji area and the Cairo view shows the Giza plateau.
